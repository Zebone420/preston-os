   (--env-file /etc/n8n.env) while the file was still EMPTY
   (install -m 600 /dev/null /etc/n8n.env earlier in runcmd).

   "docker restart n8n" never re-reads it. Workflow 1 $env
   references would resolve empty -> the live bracket could not
   have passed as-is.
